# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in Mvua to be a good experience for everyone, especially farmers' advocates, first time open source contributors, and members of the communities this project serves. Behave like a professional among peers:

- Be respectful in discussion and review; critique code, not people.
- Assume good faith; ask before accusing.
- Keep discussions on topic and in the open (public issues and PRs).
- No harassment, discrimination, trolling, or publishing of others' private information.
- Remember the humans on the other side, including the farmers this protocol exists for.

## Scope

This applies to the repository (issues, PRs, discussions), and to project spaces such as community chats and events representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this standard, and may temporarily or permanently ban repeat or serious offenders. Reports of violations go to the maintainers privately (via GitHub private vulnerability reporting contact or a direct message to a maintainer); reports are handled confidentially.
